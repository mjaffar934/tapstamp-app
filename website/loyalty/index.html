Why shops switch

Same idea as a paper punch card — fewer holes in the process.

  • They already have Wallet open

    Apple Pay and Google Pay mean the wallet is daily habit. Your loyalty card sits next to their cards — not buried in a random app they forget to reopen.

  • Stamps and rewards push to the lock screen

    When you stamp them, the pass updates. When a free coffee or treatment unlocks, they get a notification. You don’t rely on them remembering a half-full paper card.

  • No customer app download

    They tap once and “Add to Wallet.” Staff use the TapStamp owner app at the till — customers never create an account with you unless you ask for email.

  • Hardware cost is £0 for loyalty

    The NFC stamp that lives on your counter ships free with the programme. Google Reviews and Instagram NFC are separate products — buy them only if you want those extras.
