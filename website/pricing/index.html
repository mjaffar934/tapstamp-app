Questions

Is it really £0 to start?

Yes. £0 today — stamp included free. We save a card at signup. Software stays free for your first 50 unique customers each calendar month. You only pay £25/mo when you go over 50.

When do I get charged?

Not at signup. The first charge is when you hit 50 unique customers in a month with a card on file — then £25/mo. Under 50, software stays free. The counter resets on the 1st.

Do customers need to download an app?

No. They tap your stamp and add a pass to Apple Wallet or Google Wallet.

What counts as one unique customer?

One person who receives at least one stamp in a calendar month. Five visits from the same person still counts as one. The counter resets on the 1st.
