FAQ

Do I need to set anything up?

No. Tell us your Google review link or Instagram @ at checkout (or just your business name and address for Google). We program the NFC before it ships — ready to use when it arrives.

I don’t have a Google review link

No problem. Enter your business name and address at checkout and we’ll find the correct Google review / Maps link and program it for you.

What is free London drop-off?

Choose Free London drop-off at checkout if you’re in Greater London. We’ll arrange hand delivery instead of posting. Elsewhere in the UK, delivery is free by post (next-day free when you buy two or more).

Apple Pay / Google Pay?

Yes — checkout runs on Stripe. Apple Pay, Google Pay, Link, and cards are available where your device supports them.

Can you change the link after I order?

Usually yes if it hasn’t shipped. Email support@tapstamp.co with your order email and the new URL or @.

Stand vs professional NFC?

Stands sit on the till (£18.99). Professional NFC is the domed epoxy disc you stick on a counter, window, or packaging (£14.99). Same programming either way.
